## 2026-11-12: PLAN-007 drafted: ADR-006 lands as S21 then S22 after S17 and S11b; S18 waits for S22

- **Decided by:** the Technical Lead on TICK-736, from ADR-006 (accepted on TICK-714). Plan: `docs/delivery/plan-adr-006.md`.
- **Decision:** feature TICK-738 with two serial stories. S21 (TICK-739): registry, four typed side tables, trigger and coercion, the flag at publish with the default quota, the snapshot barrier and backfill, the compiled filters, measured against D13. S22 (TICK-740): removal, the operator raise to 10 through an audited `tenant_limits` row, seed types flagged and the JSONB scan retired.
- **Sequencing:** S21 starts after S17 (TICK-664) and S11b (TICK-337) merge into `main` (shared registry, grants, outbox, relay, worker). S22 waits for S21. S18 (TICK-669) now also waits for S22, because they share publish, grants and the registry; if the human wants numbering formats first, the order flips and nothing else changes.
- **Interim behaviour:** in S21 a flag removal is refused (422 `filterable_field_removal_unavailable`), and the legacy JSONB scan is kept only for a fixed allow-list of seed fields (`fieldindex.LegacyScanFields`) until S22 removes it.
- **Error codes pinned:** `filterable_field_removal_unavailable`, `searchable_not_available`.
- **Deferred:** free-text search (RFC-016), sort on data fields, the adaptive statement shape (trigger: R-a), a UI, a per-tenant non-coercion report (trigger: R5).
- **Dissent:** none.
