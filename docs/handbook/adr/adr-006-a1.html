    ADR-006-A1: the D13 350 ms residual follows the fan-out, not the ACL

    Status: ACCEPTED, rev 1, 2026-11-20 (Solution Architect verdict, TICK-1001), after the Technical Architect's round-1 critique. Every finding was taken. Nothing was contested, so the CTO did not need to break a tie. This page amends ADR-006 D13 and residual R-a. Normative text: the A1 record. Explainer: A1-explained.html. Evidence: a1-d13-bands. The page is in-progress because ADR-006's implementation is not on main yet.

    Paraphrases: "What was measured", A1-1 to A1-8.

    Problem

    TICK-992 measured a 400k-record type with 8 clients on the reference machine. A data filter matching 2% of the type ran at 236.6–267.6 ms p95 without a row ACL. D13 allowed 350 ms only with a narrowing ACL. Without an ACL, the page drives from the side index: 8,098 ids, then 8,098 records_pkey lookups and a top-N sort. With an ACL, it scans the 24,118 ACL-visible records instead. A 40% filter with an ACL ran at 176.1–327.6 ms. In that case the capped count hash-joins all 160,258 side rows of the value, and the hash spills at work_mem = 4 MB. The ACL was the wrong proxy. The real cost is a fan-out proportional to the matches or to the visible set.

    Decision

    • A1-1 The two bands at ≤ 350 ms p95:

      • (i) 0.5–2% of the type, with or without a narrowing ACL. With an ACL, this holds up to the measured breadth: the agent sees 6% of the type.
      • (ii) ≥ 40% with a narrowing ACL, at work_mem = 4 MB and about 160k side rows per value.

      Every other band stays at 150 ms. The 2–40% range is unmeasured: its target stays 150 ms, and it is scored as unmeasured, not as passing.

    • A1-2 A band is extended only by a measured cell together with its plan, never by a latency number or an extrapolation.

    • A1-3 R-a is rewritten for both bands. The count-only clause is dropped.

    • A1-4 Trigger: the histogram nava_records_list_data_filter_seconds, with the single label row_acl. The alert fires when more than 1% of requests take over 350 ms for 1 h. A slow-list log line (tenant_id, entity_definition_id, operator, row_acl, count, duration; no value or field name) confirms that a single tenant is affected. The Technical Lead owns this follow-up task.

    • A1-5 The 0.25% in near-misses (160.9 / 152.8 ms, no fan-out) keep 150 ms. They are recorded as misses on a contended host.

    • A1-6 Spike scope for the adaptive shape, option (a). The compiler must force the per-record probe for the count and the order-index walk for the page, with no enable_* and no hints. The spike also covers adversarial fixtures: values anti-correlated with updated_at, and a narrow ACL whose count stays under the cap. Exit: both bands at ≤ 150 ms.

    • A1-7 Spike S1 measures 5%, 10% and 20%. Spike S2 measures 2% at ACL breadths of 6%, 25% and 50%. Both run in TICK-1002 or TICK-749 and do not gate S21.

    • A1-8 317.6 and 327.6 ms are provisional passes against 350 ms.

    Options that lost

    • (b) Promoted columns: the DDL is per field and hard to reverse.
    • Holding S21 until (a) ships: (a) has not been measured.
    • Rerunning on an idle host: the single-client time is already 84–97 ms.
    • Keeping 0.5–5%: it was not measured, and is predicted at about 590–670 ms.
    • The count_capped label: it misses 2% + ACL, where the count is 487.
    • Raising every band to 350 ms.
