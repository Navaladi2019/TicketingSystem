              ADR-004-E1: cascade audit entries and diff_truncated

              Status on main: ACCEPTED (rev 2), 2026-11-02, by the Solution Architect; nothing contested. Erratum to ADR-004: replaces the R2 action list and the R1 envelope list, amends R23 and adds acceptance test CI-A17. Normative text: the E1 record; explainer: E1-explained.html.

              Paraphrases: "Decision" items 1 and 2, "Consequences".

              Problem

              Two rows required by the cascade rules (ADR-001-A5 and the records-path plan) could not be expressed in ADR-004's vocabulary. Separately, a diff over 64 KB had no way to say it was cut.

              Decision

              Item 1, cascade entries (new R2a).

              • action gains cascade_cycle and cascade_too_deep; subject_type gains cascade_key.
              • A cycle entry is written in the write's transaction, once per suppressed start. A too-deep entry is written on the pipeline's own transaction runner and commits independently of the rejected command.
              • The cascade key is a fixed-namespace UUIDv5 over tenant, origin, record and definition, computed in Go by both the writer and the verifier.
              • At most one cycle entry per origin, record and definition per write, so the unique key cannot roll back the user's write.
              • Neither entry ever carries the attempted record data or an idempotency key.

              Item 2, diff_truncated.

              • The envelope gains a boolean diff_truncated, always emitted, inside the chain, so flipping it is detectable.
              • Past 64 KB the stored diff keeps whole RFC 6902 operations only; secrets are checked on the full diff before the cut.
              • audit/erase entries are never truncated: the cap is at most 1,500 entry ids per erase entry, and an oversize one is refused, so erasure cannot silently leave entries that verify as tampered.

              No migration or backfill was needed; the table was empty when this was accepted.
