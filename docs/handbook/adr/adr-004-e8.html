              ADR-004-E8: the reader also reads the chain columns

              Status on main: ACCEPTED, 2026-11-08, by the Solution Architect after the Technical Architect's critique and Security's approval on the PR; not contested. Erratum to ADR-004-A1 A1-1 and A1-7 only. It corrects an omission and changes no rule. Normative text: the E8 record; explainer: E8-explained.html.

              Paraphrases: "Context", "Decision", "Why these three columns are safe".

              Problem

              A1 puts the export pages on the nava_audit_reader pool, and the export format needs the chain links and the erasure reference. But A1-1's column list omitted prev_hash, entry_hash and erasure_ref, so an export page failed with a permission error. A1 also did not say where the export's head sequence and hash come from.

              Decision

              • The reader's audit_log grant gains prev_hash, entry_hash and erasure_ref, 30 columns in all. txid stays out.
              • The three columns reveal nothing new: the hashes are derived from columns the reader already reads and are published by the export, and the erasure reference equals the matching erase entry's subject id.
              • The head read for an export is the lease statement itself on nava_app, returning the head seq and entry_hash; zero rows means 409 export_in_progress. The reader gets no grant on audit_chain_head.
              • The lease transaction commits before the first page is read, so the head row lock is never held while paging.
