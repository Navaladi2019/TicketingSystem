              S15: Each tenant's audit entries are sealed into a hash chain

              Story TICK-420 (kit TICK-443, timings updated for ADR-004-E7). Verdict READY, 2026-11-07, recorded in the acceptance kit. All four criteria and the isolation check pass.

              What a user can now do

              • Rely on every audit entry of a tenant getting a place in that tenant's chain (seq) and a hash chained to the entry before it, without anyone doing anything.
              • Expect a busy tenant's entries to be sealed about 10 seconds after they are written. A quiet tenant is still sealed within 5 minutes of its next write, then back to about 10 seconds.
              • Lose nothing and number nothing twice if the sealing worker dies: the next worker carries on where the chain stopped.
              • Have a newly onboarded tenant sealed from its first entry on.

              How to try it

              1. sh docs/acceptance/S15/tools/upgrade-setup.sh (about 10 minutes: the stack starts just before migration B and is upgraded, so tenants A and B get a chain head).
              2. sh docs/acceptance/S15/tools/run-kit.sh: runs the Bruno folders S15, S15K (after a worker kill) and S15I (after idle), with the kill and the SQL check in between. About 20 minutes.
              Operation Bruno request file
              onboardTenant S15/S15-5.bru (a new tenant is sealed from its first entry)
              createRecord S15/S15-6.bru, S15/S15-8.bru, S15/S15-10.bru (Alice), S15/S15-12.bru (Bob), S15I/S15I-3.bru (write after idle)
              getRecordHistory S15/S15-14.bru (tenant B: 404)
              listAuditEntries S15/S15-7.bru, S15/S15-9.bru, S15/S15-11.bru (sealed within about 10 s), S15/S15-13.bru (A's chain: no gap, nothing of B), S15I/S15I-4.bru (idle tenant within 300 s), S15K/S15K-2.bru (after the kill: no gap, no duplicate)

              S15/S15-15.bru reads audit_unsealed_oldest_seconds and audit_seal_failures_total from the worker's /metrics. S15/S15-1.bru to S15/S15-4.bru, S15I/S15I-1.bru, S15I/S15I-2.bru and S15K/S15K-1.bru sign in. No API field shows sealed: a sealed entry has seq and sealed_at; the hashes are in the S16 export.

              Verdict and evidence

              • Verdict: READY, 2026-11-07, four of four criteria pass.
              • Recorded run used: the kit's committed run-summary.json (21 requests, 71 of 71 tests), from a fresh stack; seal latency is in ac1-seal-latency.txt. Not re-run for this page.
              • Known limits: no reseed of a dead chain here (that is S16); migration B needs a maintenance window; a stack created at the branch never seals the seeded tenants, hence the upgrade script.
