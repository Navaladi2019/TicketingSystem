## Problem

E4's cue to look for a uniform slowdown was an absolute warning. That is always on for a slow CPU model (one model's import baseline is already over the threshold) and blind on a fast one (a slowdown must reach more than twice the baseline before any warning). A spike showed timings on one CPU model are stable (max/min under 1.2).

## Decision

- A row is off-baseline when the run's CPU model has a recorded baseline in `docs/budgets.md` and the row is more than 1.2 times that model's recorded value.
- E4-R1 is revisited on two off-baseline `main` runs in a row on a recorded model, or a baseline re-take more than 1.2 times the previous one. Runs on unrecorded models neither count nor break the streak.
- An over-threshold baseline alone is not a regression signal; it is annotated as a slow host when its ratios match the other models.
- The check stays manual, owned by the Postgres specialist.
- A baseline re-take is the median of at least three runs on that model.

Nothing in the CI gate changes: no verdict, threshold or code.
