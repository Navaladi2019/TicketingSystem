## What is on main

| Part of the ADR | State |
|---|---|
| Everything: the `x-nava-filterable` flag, the four side tables, the trigger, the backfill and removal workflows, the quota, the `filter[f]` checks | **Accepted, not implemented. Nothing is on `main`.** |

Until it ships, a data filter is the legacy JSONB scan held by `store.DataFilterTimeout` (TICK-621: `filter[priority]=1` p95 841 ms, `filter[site]=s7` 1,287 ms). It replaces the 2026-11-09 D25 deferral of data-filter indexing (accepted by the CTO, TICK-646) and the PLAN-003 cut of range filters on data fields.

## Decision

*Paraphrases: "Decision", D1 to D14.* A tenant field marked `x-nava-filterable` is copied, by a trigger on `records` in the same transaction, into one of four typed side tables (`text`, `num`, `ts`, `bool`). Filters run as leakproof B-tree comparisons that PostgreSQL pushes below RLS for `nava_app`. `records.data` stays the only source of truth, and no DDL runs per tenant or per field (ADR-001 I8).

- A field flagged after data exists is backfilled by an outbox-started Temporal workflow behind a snapshot barrier; removal runs the same way.
- `filter[f]` answers in this order: masked is 400 `masked_field`, not flagged is 400 `field_not_filterable`, backfilling is 422 `field_not_ready`, ready runs the side-table predicate. Operators are `eq`, `in` (at most 100 values), and `lt`, `lte`, `gt`, `gte` for numbers and timestamps. Sort on data fields stays out.
- The side table is read only through one correlated `EXISTS` inside the single list statement that carries the row ACL.
- Quota: 5 filterable fields per type by default, up to 10 with operator approval, at most 50 per tenant.
- Latency target: list p95 at or under 150 ms, except mid selectivity (0.5 to 5%) with a narrowing row ACL, accepted at p95 at or under 350 ms.
- **Free-text search is not decided here.** It moves to RFC-016; `x-nava-searchable` is reserved and publish refuses it.

## Options that lost

*Paraphrases: "Rejected options".* Slot columns on `records`; promoted fixed columns; an external search store; one shared typed side table; a store-layer write instead of the trigger; a JSONB fallback scan while backfilling; a UNION-compiled ACL as the only shape; a term table for search.

## Accepted risks

*Paraphrases: "Risks and accepted residuals".* The 350 ms band (R-a); inserts with 5 flagged fields at 43 to 63% of baseline (R-b); runtime roles hold `SELECT, INSERT, DELETE` on the side tables (R8); masks must stay row-independent (R9); a long transaction delays `ready` (R11); rare low-cardinality values depend on the MCV list (R12); non-coercing values are counted, not filterable (R5); float8 beyond 15 significant digits (R3).
