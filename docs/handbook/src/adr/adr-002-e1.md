Status on `main`: **ACCEPTED**, 2026-10-28, approved by the product owner, who accepted residual R-e. Erratum to [ADR-002](adr-002.md): replaces its "Lint rule" design row and closes its residual R-b. Normative text: [the E1 record](../../../../docs/adr/ADR-002-E1-line-scoped-nolint.md); explainer: [E1-explained.html](../../../../docs/adr/ADR-002-E1-explained.html).

*Paraphrases: "Decision", "Context", "Consequences", "How a second GroupPaths caller is caught", "Rejected options", "Accepted residual".*

## Problem

ADR-002 let one handler call the group-path reader by excluding the whole of `internal/server/api.go` from a lint rule. That also switched off the file's other two rules. Security raised it as a Low finding, not exploitable today.

## Decision

- Remove the per-file exclusion.
- The one call in `GetMe` carries a line-scoped `//nolint:forbidigo` directive, word for word as in the record.
- Enable the `nolintlint` linter with specific, explained, not-unused directives.
- A CI script, run with the lint step, fails closed on three checks: the set of such directives must be exactly that one line (in any spelling); the lint config may have no new exclusion; and the only generated Go files may be the two known ones (a fake generated-file header would silence every linter).

ADR-002's acceptance-criterion row for the lint rules is reworded to include the gate. R-b is closed and replaced by R-e.

## Options that lost

Keeping the file exclusion; narrowing it by text; moving `GetMe` to its own excluded file; a directive plus a count-only gate (bypassable by moving the directive or changing its case); a function-scoped custom analyzer (the right shape, deferred until a second waiver is needed).

## Consequences

No runtime code changes. I5 is strengthened. Adding a second waiver means editing the gate, which is a reviewed CI change.

## Accepted risks

R-e: the gate pins the line text, not its location, so the line could be moved to another function in the file. Review of that file is the control. Severity Low. Revisit when a second waiver is needed or this happens once; then adopt the custom analyzer.
