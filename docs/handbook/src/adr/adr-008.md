## Decision

*Paraphrases: "Decision".* Every new ADR, amendment and erratum has a `## Competitive Gate` section. It names the axis the decision moves, with a number and a method, and the pain points from the register that it avoids or risks repeating. It also states that the invariants I1–I9 are unchanged. The Technical Architect checks the section in the critique.

| Axis | One line |
|---|---|
| X1a | First routed record, self-serve: target 8 h or less; competitor side unmeasured. |
| X1b | Production go-live of a reference service desk: compared with months of partner-led work; our side unmeasured. |
| X2 | Change and upgrade cost: one publish, no code, no tenant-authored DDL; zero items to review after an upgrade within a major version. |
| X3 | Speed: our p95 budgets; a 10x claim is unproven until competitors are timed client-side. |
| X4 | Cost to serve: the target is the human's decision. |

The rules that make the gate pass or fail are: a multiplier needs both sides measured the same way (D4); a grade-C source alone never triggers a send-back (D3); `N/A` only for internal ADRs (D7); and a platform limit must pass the five-point test in D11. The back-apply found no errata.
