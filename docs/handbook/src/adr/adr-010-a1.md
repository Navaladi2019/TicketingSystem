## Problem

SP-2 measured ADR-010's provisional caps. At 256 or more leaves, one list page costs the whole 150 ms budget. The caps counted leaves, not bytes, so a 1 MiB document inside every cap took 0.15 to 0.6 s to check on publish. And `nava_worker` could insert into `acl_versions`, but the tenant-id trigger cannot see principals as that role, and `SECURITY DEFINER` is banned (I3).

## Decision

| # | One line |
|---|---|
| A1-1 | Caps: depth 8, 16 children, 64 policies, **128 leaves per policy and per document**. |
| A1-2 | The stored format-2 `rules` is at most **32,768 bytes** of PostgreSQL's `jsonb` text. The route refuses it with 422 `document_too_large`, pointer `/rules`; `acl_versions_rules_size_check` is the backstop, and `acl2_rules_valid` skips the walk above the cap. |
| A1-3 | Only `nava_app` inserts into `acl_versions`. `nava_worker` loses INSERT, leaves the `acl_versions_insert` policy and loses EXECUTE on the CHECK functions. The trigger stays `SECURITY INVOKER`. A future worker publish path needs its own amendment. |

## Evidence

The worst 32 KiB document within the caps costs 17 to 41 ms p50 and up to about 70 ms p95 to CHECK and trigger; a realistic document at the caps costs 3.6 to 7.4 ms p50. The boundary is exact (32,768 accepted, 32,769 refused).

## Residuals

`AR-TICK-971-01` in [accepted risks](../security/accepted-risks.md): a crafted 32 KiB document costs up to about 70 ms per publish in the admin's own tenant.
