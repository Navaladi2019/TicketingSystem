## Problem

SP-2 measured ADR-010's provisional caps. At 256 or more leaves, one list page costs the whole 150 ms budget. The caps counted leaves, not bytes, so a 1 MiB document inside every cap took 0.15 to 0.6 s to check on publish, and 1 MiB of masks about 0.3 s. And `nava_worker` could insert into `acl_versions`, but the tenant-id trigger cannot see principals as that role, and `SECURITY DEFINER` is banned (I3).

## Decision

| # | One line |
|---|---|
| A1-1 | Caps: depth 8, 16 children, 64 policies, **128 leaves per policy and per document**. Not configurable. |
| A1-2 | `rules` (format 2) and `masks` are each at most `NAVA_ACL_MAX_DOCUMENT_BYTES` of PostgreSQL `jsonb` text: one operator setting for the whole installation, **default 32 KiB, range 16 to 128 KiB**, never per tenant. The route refuses with 422 `document_too_large`, pointer `/rules` or `/masks`, and reports the effective limit only there. The database backstop is the 128 KiB ceiling (`acl_versions_bytes_rules_check`, `acl_versions_bytes_masks_check`), checked before any walk. |
| A1-3 | Only `nava_app` inserts into `acl_versions`. `nava_worker` loses INSERT, leaves the `acl_versions_insert` policy and loses EXECUTE on the CHECK functions. The trigger stays `SECURITY INVOKER`. A future worker publish path needs its own amendment. |

## Evidence

The worst row that fits (unused `ref` chains plus masks at the limit) costs 23–42 ms p50 and at most about 90 ms p95 at the 32 KiB default, and 78–252 ms p50 and at most about 340 ms p95 at the 128 KiB ceiling. A realistic document at the caps costs 5 to 8 ms p50. The boundary is exact for both columns.

## Residuals

`AR-TICK-971-01` in [accepted risks](../security/accepted-risks.md): a crafted row costs up to about 90 ms per publish at the default and about 340 ms at the ceiling, in the admin's own tenant.
