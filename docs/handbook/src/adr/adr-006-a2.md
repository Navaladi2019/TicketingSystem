## Problem

For a broad filter (≥ 40%) under a narrowing row ACL, the page is fast, but the capped count is not. PostgreSQL either hashes every side row of the value or probes each visible record. Which plan it picks follows the side table's visibility map: after a backfill or after edits, the count took 100–490 ms single-client and 578–983 ms p95 on one fresh stack. Rev 0/1 tried to force the right plan with a bounded pre-count and a visible-set guard. Spike S-A2-anti failed: the right plan depends on how the value correlates with the caller's visible set, and nothing can know that before doing the work.

## Decision

- **A2-1 Contract.** `total_state: exact | capped | unknown` is required. `total` is `integer | null`, and `null` means unknown, never 0. `total_capped` is deprecated. The UI shows "1,000+" for `capped`, "N+" or "many" for `unknown`, and keeps the best-known total across pages.
- **A2-2 Budget.** `CountBudget = 200 ms`, a named constant: 350 − 94 (idle handler p95 73 ms + band (ii) page 21 ms) − 6 (two round trips) − 35 (10% margin) = 215, rounded down. The bound holds pointwise, so it holds at p95.
- **A2-3 Mechanism.** Page first, then `SAVEPOINT list_count; SET LOCAL statement_timeout`, then the one capped count statement, then `ROLLBACK TO SAVEPOINT` on both paths. A 57014 gives 200 with `unknown`. The budget is `statement_timeout`, never a context deadline. It covers every list with a data filter.
- **A2-4 First-page shortcut.** A first page with at most `limit` rows is the whole result: `total` is exact and no count runs.
- **A2-5 D7.** Unchanged shape and static test. `total` is the capped count under the budget, or unknown, or taken from a short first page.
- **A2-6 Isolation.** `unknown` is a threshold of the count's duration, already visible in latency. Security approved it and rated it Info.
- **A2-7 Metric.** One new label, `total_state`. An alert fires above 5% `unknown` over 1 h. It is expected after backfills.
- **A2-8 Merge gate.** Budget check under the host rule; spike S-A2-cancel on the real slow plan (overshoot ≤ 15 ms at p99); failure-path, tenant-context, pooled-connection and test-only-hook tests; PR #812's refusal, tenant-scope and ACL-denied tests carried over; the kit's ≥ 40% ACL cell ≤ 350 ms p95 on two fresh stacks.
- **A2-9** D13 does not change. The page half (the anti-correlated page walk, 2.2–3.4 s at 50% visibility) stays on the R-a track.

## Options that lost

- Rev 0/1, the bounded pre-count with a forced probe: failed S-A2-anti (+341–557 ms) and S-A2-multi.
- The visible-sample reshape: unbounded when the sample is biased.
- A D13 exception: it never closes by itself.
- A promoted column per field: too heavy for S21; stays on R-a.
- Leaving `total` out instead of `null`: decodes to 0 in Go.
- ANALYZE or VACUUM at the flip, `random_page_cost`, `work_mem`.
