An agent logs an incident, updates it, sees its history; a stale update is refused

Story S9, TICK-335. Kit: docs/acceptance/s9-records.md (TICK-359).

What you can test

An agent creates incidents over the API. Each gets the tenant's next number (INC0000001, INC0000002, …) and an ETag. Sending the same request again with the same Idempotency-Key gives back the same incident instead of a second one. An update must carry the ETag of the copy it was based on: an update from an out-of-date copy is refused with 412, one with no ETag at all with 428. Every change is kept in the incident's history, naming who made it. Another tenant gets the same 404 for your incident as for one that does not exist.

Known limitation, on purpose (AR-S9-01): until story S10 lands, any member of a tenant may read and write every incident of that tenant. Alice can update any tenant A incident, and there are no field-level permissions yet. This is not a bug; do not report it. Other tenants stay out. S9 is never released without S10.
The story's users are the seeded ones: agent@tenant-a is Alice (alice@tenant-a.test) and agent@tenant-b is Bob (bob@tenant-b.test).

Known limitations

AR-S9-01, on purpose: until S10, any member of a tenant may read and write every incident of that tenant. Field masking and per-record permissions come with S10. Not a bug.

The exact INC0000001 is only what a fresh stack shows. On a stack where incidents were created before, the folder checks the number's shape and that there is no gap.

Not in this story

A UI, listing or searching incidents, and record-level permissions (S10). Concurrency (many writers on one record), the cascade depth and cycle checks and the database guarantees are in CI (TestRecords*, TestPipeline*, the isolation report), not in this guide.
